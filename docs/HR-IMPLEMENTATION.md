Departments remain at `/hr/departments` and are linked from Organization. The employee form lists the standard company departments (Administration, Finance, Human Resources, Information Technology, Engineering, Operations, Sales, Marketing, Customer Support, Legal, Procurement, and Product). Missing ones are created the first time departments are loaded. Each employee row has Edit, which updates the same details and monthly salary. A hike percent and effective date on that form raises the earnings and keeps a record of the previous amounts. Payslips generated afterward use the revised salary.

## Payroll

Enter basic, house rent allowance, special allowance, leave and travel allowance, professional tax, and provident fund on the employee form or My Profile. Those monthly amounts are saved with the employee and copied onto the payslip when you generate a month as `YYYY-MM`. The company legal name, address, and default “Generated by” come from Settings → Company profile and are copied onto that payroll run. Gross pay is the sum of the earnings. Net pay is gross pay minus professional tax and provident fund. The payslip page follows a salary-slip layout: company logo and profile in the header, earnings beside deductions, net salary in words, and year-to-date totals. Download PDF saves that sheet. Generate payslips also emails each active employee who has an email address, using the mailbox configured in Settings → Contact. Email employee on a single slip sends that PDF to the same address. The logo is the one stored on Settings → Company profile.

Laptop rental proposals and the rental desk now live in CRM. See `docs/CRM-IMPLEMENTATION.md`. Assign Client remains here and is only for employee staffing.

## Permissions

`hr.view`, `hr.create`, `hr.edit`, `hr.delete`.

Accounts that already have `user.update` can use these routes before the permission catalog is reseeded.
